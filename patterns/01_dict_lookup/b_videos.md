# Videos: Pattern 01: Dict lookup

## How to use these
1. **Before the lesson:** watch the short video(s) under *The idea*. They show the pattern moving, which makes the lesson easier to follow.
2. **For each problem:** have a real go first, about 20-30 minutes. Then watch its video, close it, and write your own answer.
3. The problem videos solve the original LeetCode version, so the story and names differ from our pizza version. The method is the same.

## The idea, one step at a time
Watch these in order. Each one teaches one thing, and together they take about 17 minutes.

| Step | What you'll learn | Video | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What a Python dict is: putting a value in under a key, and getting it back out | [Python Tutorial for Beginners 5: Dictionaries](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=daefaLgNkw0) (Corey Schafer) | 10 min |
| 2 | Why checking a list again and again is slow, and why a set or dict finds things straight away | [Lists vs Sets (for lookup efficiency)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xzDEEzH8XWk) (BugBytes) | 7 min |

You don't need to know how a dict works inside (hash functions, collisions, probing) to use this pattern. If a video starts on that, skip it.
