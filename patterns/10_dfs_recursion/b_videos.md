# Videos: Pattern 10: DFS and recursion (go deep first)

## How to use these
1. **Before the lesson:** watch the short video(s) under *The idea*. They show the pattern moving, which makes the lesson easier to follow.
2. **For each problem:** have a real go first, about 20-30 minutes. Then watch its video, close it, and write your own answer.
3. The problem videos solve the original LeetCode version, so the story and names differ from our pizza version. The method is the same.

## The idea, one step at a time
Watch these in order. Each one teaches one thing, and together they take about 10 minutes.
